### Hi, I'm Mert 👋

Visual designer and creative who also codes. These days I make realistic social media reels and posts (mostly for logistics) and real estate videos with drone shots and editing. My strength is social media design, combining AI tools with Adobe to create engaging visuals. Before that I designed posters, websites, motion graphics, and illustrations, and worked on 3D and VFX in Blender. All of it shaped my visual style. On the side I build small apps and tools.

#### What I'm building
- **[NotchStatus](https://github.com/RaacTW/NotchStatus)** is a macOS menu-bar app that shows Claude Code's live status right around your notch.
- **[WeSolve+](https://wesolveapp.com)** is a mobile app.
- **[BringGo Ship](https://bringgoship.com)** is a US to Mexico shipping brand I designed and built the site for.
